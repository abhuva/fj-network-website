<div><h2 id="starting-question">What becomes possible<br>when we adapt <em>the practice?</em></h2><p class="large-copy">A ball can roll, travel along a board or move between two people. There are many ways to explore rhythm, coordination and connection.</p><p>We are interested in how a practice can respond to the person taking part: their interests, choices and ways of moving. Functional Juggling is an important influence on that work, within a wider field of inclusive circus pedagogy.</p><a class="text-link" href="approach/">Why we work this way <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a></div>
