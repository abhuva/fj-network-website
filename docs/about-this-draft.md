# A website<br><em>in the making.</em>

This is a working introduction to an emerging European network concerned with inclusive circus, movement and Functional Juggling. It exists so the team can review its identity, content and design together.

**Functional Juggling Network is a working name.** The group currently has no separate legal entity. Statements of shared principles and future work are proposals for collective review.

## Content and responsibility

Organisation descriptions are preliminary. Links to existing resources retain the distinction between project partners, guest contributors and the current working group. External websites are maintained by their respective operators.

Before a final public launch, the group needs to confirm the site’s responsible operator, contact arrangements, required legal information, content permissions and agreed wording. This page is a draft-status explanation, not a completed legal notice.

## Privacy and technology

This version is a static website made with Zensical. It uses local assets, has no analytics, tracking scripts, embedded media, forms or visitor accounts, and sets no application cookies. Your hosting provider may process connection information when serving the site; external links take you to services with their own policies.

GitHub Pages is the intended temporary review host. Search engines are asked not to index the draft. That request does not restrict access: anyone with a public preview link can still visit and share it.

## Accessibility

The draft supports keyboard navigation, a skip link and layouts that adapt to smaller screens. It avoids autoplay and motion effects. These are implementation choices, not a claim of certified compliance. Accessibility feedback belongs in the [team review](review.md).
