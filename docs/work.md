## A collection of practical experiences

**Let’s talk about inclusive circus practice** brings together case studies from different countries and settings. Browse the original accounts on CircusWiki, with their authors and context.

[Explore the case-study collection](https://nica-ev.github.io/circuswiki/en/Lets-talk-about-inclusive-circus-practice/){ .button }

## The project behind the publication

The 2025 publication was developed through **Creative Inclusion of Special Need Groups – Much More Than Circus**, Erasmus+ project **2024-1-HU01-KA210-VET-000251194**.

The project’s own account names Hungarian Juggling Association / Inspirál, MONOKYKLO, Odskocznia Studio, Circusatelier Woesh and SELF A.P.S. as participating organisations. NICA e.V., Quat Props and Hula Hoop Integral contributed as guests. These roles describe that project, rather than the composition of today’s working group.

[Read the original project information, funding statement and credits](https://nica-ev.github.io/circuswiki/en/Disclaimer/).

## Earlier connections

Functional Juggling training and international exchanges predate this publication. One publicly documented example is the Erasmus+ Educircation workshop at CIRQUEON in September 2018, led by Craig Quat, Katka Klusáková and Adam Jarchovský.

[View CIRQUEON’s workshop account](https://cirqueon.cz/multimedia/functional-juggling-workshop-s-craigem-quatem).
