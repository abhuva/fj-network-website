# Help shape<br><em>our shared introduction.</em>

<p class="page-lead">This draft gives us something concrete to discuss. Its name, positioning and statements are proposals.</p>

## 1. What should lead our identity?

The current design introduces inclusive circus and movement, with Functional Juggling as a central influence. The working name is **Functional Juggling Network**.

Does that combination describe us? Would we prefer a broader name for the network, while keeping Functional Juggling prominent in our story? What should a visitor understand in their first minute?

## 2. Who is represented, and how?

Check [the network overview](network.md): names, spelling, organisational affiliations and descriptions. Are people participating personally, representing an organisation, or both? Who else should be involved in this conversation?

## 3. What can we say together?

Review the [proposed principles](approach.md). Which commitments do we already share? Which still need discussion? Who can answer enquiries and speak on behalf of the group?

## 4. What work should visitors see first?

Choose one example per organisation. A useful contribution would include a short account of the setting, the approach, what happened, the author and a link to further information. Confirm permissions for any images and participant accounts.

## 5. How open should our resources be?

Agree licences for original texts, data and teaching materials, a process for contributions, and responsibility for maintenance. [Open practice](open-practice.md) distinguishes what this draft implements from what still needs collective agreement.

## Design questions

- Does the warm, editorial style feel like our practice and our ambitions?
- Does the language welcome newcomers while giving professional partners enough substance?
- Which languages should we prioritise after the English draft?
- Which real practice images can we use with appropriate consent, credit and descriptions?

For now, share feedback in the team’s existing conversation, or [contact a participating organisation](connect.md). This page does not collect or submit responses.
