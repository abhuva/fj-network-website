# A question is<br><em>a good place to start.</em>

<p class="page-lead">You might be developing a workshop, looking for a practice exchange, or wondering how our work connects with yours.</p>

## What would you like to explore?

- **Exchange practice:** share an activity, a question from your setting or an experience you would like to discuss.
- **Develop a collaboration:** explore a training, meeting or project with people working in different countries.
- **Connect networks:** discuss how this emerging group could contribute to the wider inclusive circus field.
- **Contribute knowledge:** help document a method, improve a resource or clarify its reuse terms.
